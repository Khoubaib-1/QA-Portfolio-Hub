# Day 1 Study Log: Computer Science & Scrum Foundations

**Date:** November 2, 2026  
**Focus Area:** Software QA Architecture, SDLC vs. STLC, Agile/Scrum Framework

---

## 📌 Key Takeaways
- **Shift-Left Testing:** Integrating QA early in the SDLC (during requirements/planning) drastically reduces defect remediation cost.
- **Scrum Ceremonies for QA:** 
  - *Sprint Planning:* Review User Stories for testability, define Acceptance Criteria, and establish Definition of Done (DoD).
  - *Daily Standup:* Track test execution progress, communicate critical bug blockers.

---

## 🎯 Daily Interview Practice
- **Q:** At what stage does QA join the Scrum process?
- **A:** Day 0 (Sprint Planning / Backlog Refinement). QA ensures Acceptance Criteria are clear and testable before development begins.
